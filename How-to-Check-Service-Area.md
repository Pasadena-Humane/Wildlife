# Step 1: Visit LA's Precincts Maps

Go to https://www.lavote.gov/apps/precinctsmaps. 

# Step 2: Search by Address

Look underneath the title "Use this application to look up your district and precinct maps.", click the drop-down, and select `District Map Look Up by Address`.

![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b78c1ea5-f4ca-4144-8106-836121cfb23a)

Fill in:

- Home Number (numbers only, no fractions)
- Street Name (do not include direction or street type)

Click `Submit`.

![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a7a8d82f-60f8-466a-8f69-db1a4653f803)

# Step 3: Check Results
