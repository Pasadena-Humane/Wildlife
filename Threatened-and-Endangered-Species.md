## Desert tortoise
- 8-15 in. in shell length

## Barefoot gecko
- Alternate names: Switak's banded gecko, barefoot banded gecko
- 2-3.5 in. long from snout to vent
- largest native gecko species in CA

## Alameda whipsnake
- 3-4 ft. long
- lack of a stripe down the middle of the back can help distinguish it from several sympatric garter snake species

## Light-footed Ridgway's rail
- Formerly light-footed clapper rail
- hen-sized marsh bird that is long-legged, long-toed, and approx. 14 in. long
- slightly down-curved beak and a short, upturned tail

## Yuma Ridgway's rail
- formerly Yuma clapper rail
- smaller subspecies of the Ridgway's rail
- approx. 8 inches tall
- coloring light grey to dark brown on the upper body a tawny-orange breast, and orange legs
- beak is long, and curves slightly downwards
